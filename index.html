Biography

Professionally known as Marten212, Marten Adham Alaskandrany is an Egyptian university student, editor, poet, and digital creator. He is known within the Fake World community for his literary works, creative projects, and long-standing presence since 2020. He is the founder of a WhatsApp poetry channel dedicated to publishing his original writings, the creator of an AI-based personalized sticker concept for community characters, and the founder of the Musameh Kareem Room, a community initiative focused on reconnecting people after conflicts. His interests include video editing, poetry, music, gaming, and learning German.
